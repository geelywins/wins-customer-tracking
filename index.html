The "unit position" (1st, 2nd, 3rd...) is shared across all qualifying deliveries that month, combined chronologically by DO date — regardless of which model. Each unit's DO base still uses its own model's rate at that combined position, and each model's Unit Incentive tier is looked up at the combined position of that model's last delivery for the month. Coolray and other models aren't included yet.

How it's calculated — example

If this month you deliver 2× EX2, then 1× Starray, then 1× EX5 (in that order): the EX2 units take combined positions 1–2 (their own rate: Rp350rb + Rp450rb, plus their own tier at position 2 = Rp710rb). The Starray and EX5 units continue the count at positions 3–4 (their own rate: Rp2.500rb + Rp2.500rb, plus their own tier at position 4 = Rp1.920rb). Total = Rp8.430.000.
